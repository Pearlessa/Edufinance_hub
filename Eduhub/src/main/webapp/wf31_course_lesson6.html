What is interest, really?

Understand how money grows (and how debt grows against you).

Why it matters

Interest is the single most important idea in personal finance. It either works for you (savings) or against you (debt).

In plain language

Interest is what someone pays for using money over time. When you save, the bank pays you interest. When you borrow, you pay them interest.

Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. Year 2 grows from a bigger base than year 1.

The biggest factor in compound interest isn't how much you save, it's how EARLY you start. R200/month from age 20 beats R500/month from age 30.
