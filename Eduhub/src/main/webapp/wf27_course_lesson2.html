Build your first monthly budget

A 3-step plan to know exactly where your money goes each month.

Why it matters

A budget isn't about saying no — it's about deciding where your money goes BEFORE it disappears.

In plain language

Step 1 — Add up everything coming in this month. Be honest, not hopeful.

Step 2 — Write your fixed costs at the top: rent, data, transport pass. These don't move.

Step 3 — What's left is what you actually have to play with. Split it into food, savings, and 'fun money'. On Sunday evenings, take 5 minutes to check what you spent. No judgement — just adjust next week's plan.
