Budget Planning in 3 simple steps

A visual walk-through: see your money, plan it, then track it — no spreadsheets needed.

Why it matters

Budgeting feels boring until you realise it's the difference between feeling broke and feeling in control.

In plain language

SEE IT — Write down every source of money coming in this month: stipend, allowances, side income.

PLAN IT — Decide where each rand goes beforehand. Needs first, then savings, then wants.

TRACK IT — Once a week, check what you actually spent vs what you planned.
