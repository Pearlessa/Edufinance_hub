Tax-free savings for under-25s

How a TFSA works and why opening one in your 20s is a cheat code.

Why it matters

A Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) means SARS never takes a cent from your growth.

In plain language

A TFSA is a special account you can open at any major SA bank or investment platform.

You can put in up to R36,000 per year, with a R500,000 lifetime cap.

It works best as a long-term account: shares or unit trusts inside it grow much more than cash over 10+ years.
