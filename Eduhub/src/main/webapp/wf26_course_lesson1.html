The 50/30/20 rule explained simply

+20 XP Beginner Budgeting Basics

Split every rand you get into Needs, Wants and Savings — without an app.

Why it matters

Most students run out of money before month-end because nothing tells them how much is 'too much'. The 50/30/20 rule is a simple guard-rail you can do in your head.

In plain language

Take any money that lands in your account — stipend, side gig, allowance — and imagine cutting it into three pieces.

Half (50%) goes to NEEDS: things you can't skip like rent, data, transport and basic food. If your needs are taking more than 50%, that's the first place to cut.

Just under a third (30%) is for WANTS: takeaways, a night out, new clothes. This is your 'guilt-free' money — when it's done, it's done.

The last 20% goes straight to SAVINGS or to pay off debt. Move it the same day your stipend lands, before you can spend it.
