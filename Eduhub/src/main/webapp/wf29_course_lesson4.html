Debit vs credit: what's actually safer?

Understand the real difference before you sign up for any card.

Why it matters

Banks make a lot of money from students who don't understand the difference. Knowing it puts you in control.

In plain language

A DEBIT card spends money you already have. When the account is empty, it stops. No debt possible.

A CREDIT card spends the BANK'S money. You pay it back later and if you don't pay in full, you're charged 20%+ interest per year.

For students, a free debit account is almost always the right choice. Credit only makes sense if you can pay the full balance every single month.
