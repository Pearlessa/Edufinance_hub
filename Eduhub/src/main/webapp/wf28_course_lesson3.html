Write a motivation letter that wins money

The simple 3-paragraph structure used by funded students.

Why it matters

Funders read hundreds of letters. A clear, honest 3-paragraph letter beats a long, dramatic one every time.

In plain language

Paragraph 1 — Introduce yourself in 2-3 lines: name, where you're from, what you're studying and why.

Paragraph 2 — Be honest about your situation. Who do you live with, what does your family earn, what would funding mean for them?

Paragraph 3 — Show direction. What do you want to do after graduating, and why does THIS funder fit your goals? Mention them by name.
