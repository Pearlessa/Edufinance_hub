Avoid hidden bank fees

The 4 charges most students don't notice — and how to switch them off.

Why it matters

Bank fees are small per swipe but can quietly take R100+ a month — that's R1,200 a year for nothing.

In plain language

Monthly account fee — switch to a free student or youth account if you haven't already.

ATM fees — drawing cash at another bank's ATM costs extra. Use your own bank or tap your card directly.

SMS notification fees — about R0.50 each. Switch to free app push-notifications instead.
