BURSARY TIPS

Apply early

Submit weeks before the deadline. Servers crash on the last day.

Get docs ready

ID, matric results, proof of income and acceptance letter — keep them in your Vault.

Tailor your motivation

Mention the bursary by name and link your goals to their values.

Apply to many

Aim for 5–10 bursaries. More applications = better odds.

Avoid scams

Never pay to apply. Real bursaries are always free.
