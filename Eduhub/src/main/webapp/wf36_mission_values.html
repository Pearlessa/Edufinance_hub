OUR MISSION

Breaking Down Financial Barriers to Education

Every year, thousands of talented South African students miss out on educational opportunities simply because they don't know about available funding or struggle to navigate the complex application processes.

We built EduFinance Hub to change that. By combining smart technology with a deep understanding of the South African education landscape, we're making it easier than ever for students to find, apply for, and manage their funding.

● Connect students with suitable bursaries and scholarships
● Provide tools for better financial management
● Build a supportive community of learners
● Teach essential financial literacy skills
